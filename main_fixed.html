Dear Khalfan,

At first, I never thought I would fall for you. In my eyes, you were just a cute, good friend — the one who always had something to say to me, even when other people thought I was cold hearted.

If you ask me when this feeling started to grow, the only answer I can give is that love grew as I felt more and more comfortable around you. I don't know the exact moment, but slowly I realized I was waiting for your texts, waiting for your replies, and looking out for every update about you, even when it wasn't in our personal chat.

As time passed, I realized it's because you are the only person who truly understands me. You understand how I act, and you never saw me as hard to get or nonchalant. You managed to reach a side of me I didn't even realize I still had.

You made me feel what love feels like again. You brighten my days so easily with just a simple text. You cheer me up when I'm at my lowest, and I'm genuinely grateful for that.

I know I've made mistakes and caused you sadness, anger, and tears — yet you still forgive me and give us a chance to grow better together.

I'm so sorry it took me this long to take this step. I've made you wait so much longer than you ever deserved, and I hate myself for keeping you waiting. I can't bear the thought of wasting another second without you by my side.

With love,

Arthur J.
